The opportunity

Testing access that congested airports can't offer

Aviation and aerospace are undergoing a fundamental transformation — driven by decarbonisation mandates, autonomous aerial mobility, and the digital overhaul of airport operations.

Yet the startups building these technologies face a critical bottleneck: access to real operational environments for testing and validation. Congested international airports cannot accommodate early-stage pilots, and purpose-built test facilities are expensive and isolated.

Lleida-Alguaire is the only site in southern Europe combining an uncongested commercial airfield, active MRO hangars, a green hydrogen electrolyser, EASA-certified vertiports, a 500 kN rocket test bench, a residential pilot training academy, and full 5G connectivity.

About the open call

A structured path from pilot to commercial agreement

The Aeroport de Lleida Startup Challenge is organised by Aeroports de Catalunya. It selects 5 high-potential startups and connects them to Lleida-Alguaire Airport's unique infrastructure for a focused proof-of-concept programme.

Selected startups receive dedicated workspace, a committed co-funding budget per startup, direct regulatory navigation support for EASA and SORA certifications, and up to 12 months of free fiscal and legal domiciliation in Spain for international teams.

The programme culminates in a public Demo Day at the Fira Aeronautica de Lleida, 2–3 October 2026, where the five companies present their results to press, industry partners, and sector stakeholders.

Who's behind it

Public-backed, institutionally grounded

Aeroports de Catalunya is the publicly backed operator of Catalan airports, including Lleida-Alguaire. Their institutional backing provides program continuity, direct access to airport infrastructure and operations teams, and the regulatory standing to support certifications and airspace coordination that would otherwise take months or years for a startup to navigate alone.

Plug and Play brings global deal flow, startup selection expertise, and a network of corporate partners and investors that amplifies what the programme can offer.

What you get

Program benefits

Everything a hardware-intensive startup needs to run a meaningful proof-of-concept — without the usual bureaucratic overhead.

Dedicated workspace

Co-working desks and access to MRO hangar facilities at Lleida-Alguaire Airport for the full duration of the PoC programme.

Committed PoC co-funding

A reserved budget per startup, co-funded by Aeroports de Catalunya, alongside an assigned internal airport sponsor to support your work on-site.

Free fiscal domiciliation

Up to 12 months of free legal and fiscal address registration in Spain for international startups — removing a major administrative barrier.

Regulatory navigation

Direct support to obtain EASA certifications, SORA risk assessments for drone operations, and any permits required to test in active airspace.

Ecosystem access

Introductions to Aeroports de Catalunya's operational partners (Servitec, Aeronpark, JAS, BAA Training, CESDA, Pangea Aerospace) and Plug and Play's global investor network.

Demo Day at Lleida Air Challenge

A ~10-minute public pitch in front of press, industry partners, and sector stakeholders on 2–3 October 2026 at the Fira Aeronautica de Lleida.

Pathway to commercial engagement

The PoC framework is structured as a pathway to a longer-term commercial agreement with the airport — subject to PoC results and bilateral agreement.

Eligibility

Who we're looking for

We welcome applications from startups anywhere in the world (preferably from Europe). For a hardware-intensive startup in aerospace, aviation, agritech, or defence, the single most valuable asset is a real place to test — with real operators, real airspace, and real institutional support.

Startups that have already completed a similar PoC at another airport or infrastructure site are encouraged to apply.

Target sectors

Solutions may include

Eight focus areas where Lleida-Alguaire's infrastructure creates a genuine testing advantage.

Autonomous drones, eVTOL aircraft, UAM platforms, counter-UAS, drone logistics, airspace management (UTM/U-space), and BVLOS operations requiring active airspace access.
Green hydrogen production, storage, and fuelling systems; SAF blending and handling; zero-emission ground support equipment. On-site electrolyser provides a live testing environment.
Micro-launchers, rocket propulsion testing, small satellite systems, and aerospace manufacturing. Lleida-Alguaire's 500 kN rocket test bench is available on-site.
Dual-use technologies applicable in both civilian and defence contexts — including surveillance, border monitoring, secure communications, and resilient infrastructure systems.
Precision agriculture via aerial monitoring, crop health sensing, drone-based spraying, and satellite-derived field analytics in the agricultural land surrounding the airport.
AR/VR-assisted maintenance, predictive MRO, digital twins for aircraft and ground support equipment. Active MRO hangars available for live environment testing.
Passenger experience, smart check-in, biometrics, baggage tracking, operational efficiency software, and digital infrastructure for smaller regional airports.
Simulation, e-learning, and next-gen training platforms for pilots, ground crews, and aviation technicians. The on-site residential pilot training academy is a built-in partner and testing environment.

For a hardware-intensive startup in aerospace, aviation, agritech, or defence, the single most valuable asset is a real place to test — with real operators, real airspace, and real institutional support. Lleida-Alguaire offers exactly that, at a scale and accessibility no congested international hub can match.
